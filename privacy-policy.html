# Privacy Policy for LPU - Snail **Last Updated:** August 1, 2026 LPU - Snail ("the Extension") is a browser extension developed to simplify the login process for the Lovely Professional University (LPU) WiFi portal by securely storing user credentials locally and automatically logging users into the network. By using the Extension, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. --- ## Information We Collect The Extension stores the following information locally on your device: - LPU WiFi username - LPU WiFi password - Extension preferences and settings The Extension does **not** collect, transmit, or store this information on any external server. --- ## How Your Information Is Used The stored credentials are used solely to: - Automatically detect the LPU WiFi login page. - Populate the login form with your saved credentials. - Submit the login request on your behalf. The Extension does not use your information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking. --- ## Data Storage All data is stored locally using the Chrome Extension Storage API. Your credentials remain on your device and are never uploaded to the developer or any third-party server. --- ## Data Sharing The Extension does not: - Sell your information. - Share your information with third parties. - Transfer your information to external services except the official LPU WiFi authentication portal during the login process. --- ## Permissions The Extension requests only the permissions necessary to provide its functionality: - **Storage** – To securely store your login credentials and preferences locally. - **Host Permissions** – To access the LPU WiFi authentication page and perform the automatic login process. - **Scripting** – To populate and submit the login form on the authentication page. - **Active Tab** – To detect when the LPU WiFi login page is open and initiate the login process. These permissions are used only for the stated purpose. --- ## Third-Party Services The Extension communicates only with the official LPU WiFi authentication portal to complete the login process. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are integrated into the Extension. --- ## Data Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect your stored credentials. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. --- ## Children's Privacy The Extension is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. --- ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. --- ## Contact If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Extension, please contact: **Email:** your-email@example.com --- ## Disclaimer LPU - Snail is an independent browser extension developed to improve the user experience of logging into the LPU WiFi portal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with Lovely Professional University (LPU).
